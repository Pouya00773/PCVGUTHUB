# Part 3: Discussion (in pairs) – Phrasen + Argumente

In Part 3 diskutierst du mit einem Partner. **Wichtig:** Hier zählt nicht nur WAS du sagst,
sondern dass ihr ein echtes Gespräch führt – aufeinander eingehen, nachfragen, den Partner
einbeziehen. Lieber 3 einfache Argumente flüssig bringen als 10 auswendig herunterrattern.

---

## 🗣️ Phrasenbank – diese Sätze auswendig lernen!

### Die Diskussion eröffnen
- *Shall we start with the advantages?* – Sollen wir mit den Vorteilen anfangen?
- *Let's begin with…* – Fangen wir an mit…
- *In my opinion, …* / *I think that…* – Meiner Meinung nach…

### Meinung äußern
- *I believe that…* – Ich glaube, dass…
- *From my point of view, …* – Aus meiner Sicht…
- *For me, the most important point is…* – Für mich ist der wichtigste Punkt…

### Zustimmen
- *I totally agree with you.* – Ich stimme dir völlig zu.
- *That's a good point.* – Das ist ein guter Punkt.
- *Exactly, and I would like to add that…* – Genau, und ich möchte hinzufügen…

### Höflich widersprechen
- *I see your point, but…* – Ich verstehe deinen Punkt, aber…
- *That's true, but on the other hand…* – Das stimmt, aber andererseits…
- *I'm not so sure about that, because…* – Da bin ich mir nicht so sicher, weil…

### Den Partner einbeziehen (sehr wichtig für gute Noten!)
- *What do you think?* – Was denkst du?
- *Do you agree?* – Stimmst du zu?
- *What's your opinion on this?* – Was ist deine Meinung dazu?
- *Have you ever experienced that?* – Hast du das schon mal erlebt?

### Nachfragen / Zeit gewinnen
- *Could you say that again, please?* – Kannst du das bitte wiederholen?
- *What do you mean by that?* – Was meinst du damit?
- *That's an interesting question. Well, …* – Das ist eine interessante Frage. Also…
- *Let me think about that for a moment.* – Lass mich kurz nachdenken.

### Die Diskussion abschließen
- *To sum up, we can say that…* – Zusammenfassend können wir sagen…
- *All in all, I think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.* – Alles in allem überwiegen die Vorteile.
- *So we both agree that…* – Wir sind uns also einig, dass…

## Thema 1: Being a member state of the EU – advantages and disadvantages

*(Argumente kennst du schon aus Part 2, Themen 1–4!)*

| ✅ Advantages | ❌ Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| **Peace and stability** – no war between member states for 70+ years | Member states must **give up some national control** – many laws come from Brussels |
| **Single market** – free trade, no customs duties inside the EU | **Membership costs money** – rich countries pay more than they get back |
| Citizens can **live, work and study** in any member state (e.g. Erasmus) | A lot of **bureaucracy** and slow decisions (27 states must agree) |
| **Common currency** (Euro) makes trade and travel easier | One country's crisis can affect all (e.g. Euro crisis) |

**Beispielsatz:** *In my opinion, the advantages outweigh the disadvantages – especially
peace and the single market. What do you think?*

## Thema 2: Primary or secondary market research – advantages and disadvantages

*(Siehe Part 2, Thema 8!)*

| Primary research (eigene neue Daten) | Secondary research (vorhandene Daten) |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Fresh and exact** data for your question | ✅ **Fast and cheap** – data already exists |
| ✅ Data belongs only to you – competitors don't have it | ✅ Good for a **first overview** of the market |
| ❌ **Expensive** and takes a lot of time | ❌ Data can be **old or not exact** for your question |
| ❌ You need experts to do it right | ❌ Competitors can use the **same data** |

**Beispielsatz:** *I would say the best solution is a combination: start with secondary
research and then do primary research for the important questions. Do you agree?*

## Thema 3: Breaking into foreign markets – advantageous or too risky?

*(Siehe Part 2, Themen 17–19!)*

| ✅ Advantageous | ❌ Too risky |
|---|---|
| **More customers** and higher sales – the home market is limited | **High costs** for market entry, transport and marketing |
| **Risk spreading** – if the home market is weak, other markets can help | **Cultural and language mistakes** can destroy the image |
| Learning from other markets, new ideas | **Political risks**: customs duties, sanctions, unstable countries |
| Economies of scale – producing more is cheaper per unit | **Exchange rate risk** and payment risk |

**Beispielsatz:** *I think it is risky, but with good market research and a local partner,
the chances are bigger than the risks. What's your opinion?*

## Thema 4: The demographic change – more risks or chances?

*(Siehe Part 2, Thema 15!)*

| ❌ Risks | ✅ Chances |
|---|---|
| **Pension system** under pressure – fewer workers pay for more pensioners | New markets: products and services **for older people** (healthcare, travel, smart homes) |
| **Shortage of skilled workers** in many industries | **Better job chances** for young people – companies fight for them |
| Higher costs for **healthcare and care** | **Migration** can bring new workers and ideas |
| Villages and small towns lose young people | Technology (robots, AI, digitalisation) can replace missing workers |

**Beispielsatz:** *Of course there are big risks for the pension system, but I also see
chances – for example, young people like us will find jobs more easily. Do you agree?*

## Thema 5: International trading – pros and cons

*(Siehe Part 2, Themen 18–19!)*

| ✅ Pros | ❌ Cons |
|---|---|
| **Bigger choice** and lower prices for consumers | **Dependence** on other countries (e.g. energy, chips, medicine) |
| Countries can **specialise** in what they do best | **Long transport** damages the environment |
| Creates **jobs and economic growth** | Local companies can lose against **cheap imports** |
| Brings countries together – trade partners rarely fight wars | Risks: exchange rates, political conflicts, supply chain problems |

**Beispielsatz:** *All in all, I think international trade brings more advantages – but we
have learned in the last years that too much dependence on one country is dangerous.
What do you think?*

## 🎯 Taktik für die Prüfung

1. **Eröffne aktiv:** *"Shall we start with the advantages?"* – das macht sofort einen guten Eindruck.
2. **Struktur:** ein Argument nennen → kurz erklären → Partner fragen (*"What do you think?"*).
3. **Höre zu** und reagiere auf den Partner (*"That's a good point, but…"*) – das gibt Punkte!
4. **Nicht verstanden?** Kein Problem: *"Could you say that again, please?"* – das ist völlig okay.
5. **Abschluss nicht vergessen:** *"To sum up, we both agree that…"*
