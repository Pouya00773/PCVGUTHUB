# Part 1: Introduction – Antwort-Vorlagen

**So funktioniert es:** Jede Frage hat eine Vorlage mit Lücken (`___`) und ein
Beispiel (fiktiver Student, nur damit du Satzbau und Ton siehst). Fülle die Lücken
mit deinen eigenen Infos, schreibe deine Antwort einmal ab und lerne sie **laut**.

**Goldene Regel:** 3–5 einfache Sätze pro Antwort reichen. Sprich langsam und deutlich.

---

## 1. Please introduce yourself to us.

**Vorlage:**
> Good morning. My name is ___ and I am ___ years old. I live in ___.
> I am studying ___ at the HTW Berlin. I am in my ___ semester.
> In my free time I like ___.

**Beispiel:**
> Good morning. My name is Alex and I am 22 years old. I live in Berlin.
> I am studying Business Administration at the HTW Berlin. I am in my fourth semester.
> In my free time I like playing football and meeting friends.

**Vokabeln:** semester = Semester · free time = Freizeit · to introduce = vorstellen

---

## 2. What can you tell us about your studies at the HTW?

**Vorlage:**
> I am studying ___ at the HTW Berlin. The programme takes ___ semesters.
> My main subjects are ___ and ___. I especially like ___ because ___.

**Beispiel:**
> I am studying Industrial Engineering at the HTW Berlin. The programme takes seven semesters.
> My main subjects are economics, engineering and management. I especially like the mix of
> technology and business because it is very practical.

**Vokabeln:** degree programme = Studiengang · subject = Fach · practical = praxisnah

---

## 3. What are your plans for the future?

**Vorlage:**
> After my bachelor's degree, I would like to ___ (work as a ___ / do a master's degree).
> My goal is to work in the field of ___. In five years, I hope to ___.

**Beispiel:**
> After my bachelor's degree, I would like to work as a project manager.
> My goal is to work in the automotive industry. In five years, I hope to lead my own team.

**Vokabeln:** degree = Abschluss · goal = Ziel · field = Bereich

---

## 4. Have you ever applied for a job? Where would you like to apply after your studies?

**Vorlage:**
> Yes, I have applied for ___ (a part-time job / an internship / a working student position).
> After my studies, I would like to apply at ___ because ___.

**Beispiel:**
> Yes, I have applied for a working student position last year. I wrote a CV and a cover
> letter and had a job interview. After my studies, I would like to apply at a big company
> like Siemens because they offer good career opportunities.

**Vokabeln:** to apply for = sich bewerben um · cover letter = Anschreiben · job interview = Vorstellungsgespräch

---

## 5. Have you ever been abroad? Do you intend to go abroad?

**Vorlage (Ja):**
> Yes, I have been to ___. I went there for ___ (a holiday / a language course).
> In the future, I would like to ___ (do a semester abroad / work abroad) because ___.

**Vorlage (Nein):**
> No, I have not been abroad yet, but I would like to go to ___ one day.
> I think going abroad is a good way to improve your language skills and learn about
> other cultures.

**Vokabeln:** abroad = im Ausland · to intend = beabsichtigen · semester abroad = Auslandssemester

---

## 6. What foreign languages can you speak? How long have you been learning them?

**Vorlage:**
> My native language is ___. I also speak ___ and ___.
> I have been learning English for ___ years, since primary school / secondary school.
> I want to improve my English because it is important for my career.

**Vokabeln:** native language = Muttersprache · to improve = verbessern ·
**Achtung Grammatik:** "I **have been learning** English **for** ten years" (seit einer Dauer) / "**since** 2015" (seit einem Zeitpunkt)

---

## 7. Tell us about your daily routine as a student.

**Vorlage:**
> On a typical day, I get up at ___. Then I ___ (have breakfast / go to university).
> My lectures usually start at ___. In the afternoon, I ___ (study in the library / work / do sports).
> In the evening, I ___ and I usually go to bed at ___.

**Vokabeln:** daily routine = Tagesablauf · lecture = Vorlesung · library = Bibliothek

---

## 8. What work experience have you gained so far? How can companies profit from your skills?

**Vorlage:**
> I have worked as ___ at ___. There I learned ___ (how to work in a team / how to talk to customers).
> Companies can profit from this because I ___ (can work independently / know how business processes work).

**Beispiel:**
> I have worked as a working student in a small company. There I learned how to talk to
> customers and how to organise my tasks. Companies can profit from this because I can
> work independently and I understand how business processes work.

**Vokabeln:** work experience = Berufserfahrung · to gain = sammeln/erwerben · independently = selbstständig

---

## 9. What skills have you acquired at this university for your future job?

**Vorlage:**
> At the HTW I have acquired both professional and soft skills.
> I learned a lot about ___ (economics / engineering / project management).
> I also improved my soft skills, for example teamwork, time management and giving presentations.

**Vokabeln:** to acquire = erwerben · skills = Fähigkeiten · soft skills = soziale Kompetenzen

**Vorlage:**
> I would have liked to focus more on ___ (practical projects / foreign languages / IT skills).
> I think this is important because ___. Maybe I will take an extra course to learn more about it.

**Vokabeln:** to focus on = sich konzentrieren auf · practical = praktisch · extra course = Zusatzkurs

## 11. Can you recommend our university to school-leavers? Why / why not?

**Vorlage:**
> Yes, I can recommend the HTW. The professors are ___ , the studies are very practical,
> and the campus is ___. Also, Berlin is a great city for students.
> One negative point is ___ (large groups / a lot of bureaucracy), but in general I am happy here.

**Vokabeln:** to recommend = empfehlen · school-leaver = Schulabgänger · in general = im Allgemeinen

## 12. What have you learned about self-management during your studies?

**Vorlage:**
> During my studies I have learned to organise my time and to set priorities.
> Before exams, I make a study plan and start early.
> This helps me to stay calm and work effectively. I can use this in my job later, for example
> when I have to manage projects and deadlines.

**Vokabeln:** self-management = Selbstorganisation · to set priorities = Prioritäten setzen · deadline = Frist/Abgabetermin

## 13. What facilities are there on campus? How could they be improved?

**Vorlage:**
> On our campus there is a library, a canteen (called "Mensa"), computer rooms, and sports facilities.
> I think the ___ could be improved. For example, ___ (longer opening hours / more seats in the
> library / better Wi-Fi / cheaper food in the canteen).

**Vokabeln:** facilities = Einrichtungen · canteen = Mensa · opening hours = Öffnungszeiten

---

## 14. Where can students live during their studies? Pros and cons?

**Vorlage:**
> Students can live in a student residence, in a shared flat, in their own flat, or with their parents.
> A student residence is cheap and you meet many people, but the rooms are small.
> A shared flat is fun, but sometimes there are conflicts.
> Living with your parents is the cheapest option, but you have less freedom.

**Vokabeln:** student residence/dormitory = Studentenwohnheim · shared flat = WG · rent = Miete

---

## 15. How can students finance their studies?

**Vorlage:**
> There are different ways: Many students have a part-time job or work as a working student.
> Some get money from their parents. In Germany, students can also get BAföG, which is
> financial support from the state. There are also scholarships for very good students,
> and some students take a student loan.

**Vokabeln:** to finance = finanzieren · financial support = finanzielle Unterstützung · scholarship = Stipendium · student loan = Studienkredit

---

## 16. What are your favourite subjects? Why?

**Vorlage:**

**Vokabeln:** favourite subject = Lieblingsfach · numbers = Zahlen · to explain = erklären

## 17. How do you prefer to study – in a group or on your own? Why?

**Vorlage (allein):**

> But before exams, I sometimes meet with other students to discuss difficult questions.

**Vorlage (Gruppe):**
> I prefer to study in a group because we can help each other and explain things to each other.

**Vokabeln:** on my own = alleine · to concentrate = sich konzentrieren · to discuss = besprechen

## 22. What are the advantages of doing an internship abroad?

**Vorlage:**
> An internship abroad has many advantages. First, you improve your language skills every day.
> Second, you learn how people work in other countries and cultures.
> Third, it looks very good on your CV, because companies today work internationally.
> And finally, you become more independent and self-confident.

**Vokabeln:** advantage = Vorteil · CV = Lebenslauf · self-confident = selbstbewusst

**Vorlage (Teamleader):**

> But of course I also listen to the ideas of my team members.

**Vokabeln:** to take responsibility = Verantwortung übernehmen · to lead = führen · to support = unterstützen
